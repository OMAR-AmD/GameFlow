# GameFlow: Problem Statement

**Domain:** Recommendation System

We are building a personalized video game recommendation engine for Steam users. Unlike conventional systems that recommend by genre tags, **GameFlow** recommends by behavioral profile — how a player actually plays (session intensity, completion ratio, competitive tendency, narrative affinity) rather than what genres they claim to prefer. 

The system targets gamers who struggle to discover relevant titles in a catalogue of 50,000+ games. It matters because genre-based filtering fails to distinguish two players who both own an RPG but play it in fundamentally different ways — one finishes every quest, the other explores freely and moves on. GameFlow captures that distinction and produces meaningfully differentiated recommendations for each player.
